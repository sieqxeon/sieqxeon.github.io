VoiceCount

App Information

VoiceCount is a simple application that counts spoken words in real time using voice recognition.

  • Real-time voice recognition
  • Automatic word counting
  • Hands-free operation

Privacy Policy

This application does not collect personally identifiable information.

However, third-party services such as Google AdMob may collect information as described in their own privacy policies.
